## 👋🏿 About me

I’m a full-stack engineer based in **Lubumbashi, DRC**, building the systems that organisations rely on every day. My work spans interbank payments, lending, agricultural value chains, school administration, and mining operations—with teams around the world.

I enjoy turning complicated real-world workflows into software that is dependable, understandable, and pleasant to use.

## 🚀 What I build

- 🏦 **Financial infrastructure** — interbank clearing and payment systems used by 60+ banks across eight countries.
- ☀️ **Lending platforms** — loan and asset management for pay-as-you-go solar operations across the DRC.
- 🌱 **Agricultural technology** — connecting smallholder farmers with buyers, financing, and logistics.
- 🎓 **Education systems** — modernising school administration software for Danish schools.
- ⛏️ **Operations software** — role-based platforms supporting mining services from exploration to logistics.
